# Sprint 0 Retrospective (Mar 20 – Mar 25)
## What went well
- Excellent initial research phase; the team successfully consumed a massive amount of baseline data (geotechnical studies, bird monitoring, pollen reports).
- Tasks were efficiently divided among team members, preventing overlapping work.
- Team roles and responsibilities were clearly defined early on, setting a strong foundation for the hackathon.

## What could improve
- Heavy focus on individual research created slight information silos.
- Could have started analyzing the drone photos slightly earlier to better contextualize the geotechnical data.

## Action items for next sprint
- Implement quick daily syncs to share research findings across the team.
- Consolidate all baseline research to prepare targeted, high-value questions for our first stakeholder meeting with Enery.
