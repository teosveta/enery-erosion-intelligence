SUSTAINABILITY & SOIL CARBON REPORT

Tsenovo Solar Plant, Bulgaria

Report Period: Mar 2026 – Apr 2026

Introduction

This report presents findings from the AI-powered erosion monitoring system deployed at the Tsenovo Solar Plant. The platform integrates Smart Erosion Pins, Sentinel-2 satellite NDVI data, and open-source weather and soil databases to provide a comprehensive picture of site condition.

Sustainable Farming Position

Zone 3 (41.96 ha, primary focus) exhibits active rill erosion driven by a 70m elevation gradient (152m → 82m). AI analysis of Smart Pin imagery indicates bare patch expansion of approximately 12% over the monitoring period.
